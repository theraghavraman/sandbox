Objective Personality System

A source-indexed knowledge base and architecture workbench for studying OPS concepts, comparing model versions, constructing exploratory type codes and recording evidence without treating hypotheses as diagnoses.

Research status, not a verdict. The official FAQ describes nine binary coins and 512 types. An independent page describes an expanded 11-coin/2,048-configuration model. This lab preserves both claims with provenance; it does not present OPS as a clinically validated diagnostic instrument.
