Domain & infrastructure lookup

Public RDAP and DNS-over-HTTPS lookups, where browser access is allowed.

BEST EFFORT
Enter a domain name for RDAP, DNS and certificate transparency. IP address support varies by provider.
Lookups contact the selected public services directly from your browser; those services receive the query.
DNS-over-HTTPS uses Cloudflare's public endpoint; registration data uses RDAP bootstrap; certificate transparency opens a crt.sh search. Requests can fail because of CORS, network restrictions, provider policy or rate limits. No port scanning or active probing is performed.

What these results mean

Interpret results cautiously.

RDAP registration

May show registrar, registration dates, nameservers and status. Privacy services and registry policies may redact data.

DNS records

Public A, AAAA, MX, NS, TXT and CAA answers can provide infrastructure clues, but do not establish who controls a site.

Certificate transparency

Certificates can reveal hostnames that appeared in public certificate logs. A hostname is not proof that a service is live or vulnerable.

URL inspection

Parse a URL locally before opening it.

URL components will appear here. Inspection does not visit the site.
Do not open suspicious links unless you understand the risk. URL parsing is a structural check, not a malware or phishing verdict.

Safe handling checklist

Before visiting an unfamiliar source.

Check the actual hostname

Look for misspellings, confusing subdomains, punycode and unexpected ports.

Separate observation from inference

A URL's structure alone cannot establish who operates it or whether it is malicious.

Preserve provenance

Record the original URL and the time observed. For formal work, separately preserve screenshots or source files where lawful.
