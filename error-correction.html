Error Correction Studio

An interactive digital communications lab. Compare Hamming(7,4), repetition codes and raw transmission; inject random or burst errors; measure recovery, overhead, BER and CRC integrity.

Teaching lab: results are simulated locally and use simplified channel models. Hamming(7,4) corrects a single bit error per codeword; multiple errors can be miscorrected. CRC detects many corruptions but is not cryptographic authentication.
